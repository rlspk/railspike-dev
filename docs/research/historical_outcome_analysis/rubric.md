# Outcome rubric — version 1

[Entry](./README.md) · [Sources](./sources.md) · [Queues](./progress.md)

Fixed before outcome coding on 2026-10-13. This retrospective rubric extends the [original study protocol](../railspike_paper/protocol.md); the histories have already been screened, so this is not preregistration of unseen data. Unit: the census's canonical workflow ID, not a source file, test, quote or incident account. This first administrative batch is not a representative sample.

## Evidence and acceptance

For every record, compare the initiating request and applicable plan with reported delivery, verification and corrections. Use all mapped documents as the starting boundary; inspect retained commit evidence when a relevant revision is available. Record inaccessible evidence without substituting current behavior for historical behavior. A present-day check is not evidence of a historical run.

Record ordinary-entry acceptance and required recovery separately. Labels are **reported met**, **known unmet**, **unknown**, or **not applicable with reason**; mixed criteria retain individual qualifications. These labels are not independently verified software outcomes. A missing result is unknown, an explicit unperformed required check is an unmet gate, and a failing check is not automatically a user-visible defect. Distinguish focused tests, full suites, user journeys, deployment checks, environment blockers and baseline failures. Do not silently relax a literal passing gate because failures are described as pre-existing.

Evidence strength attaches to the claim, not the entire workflow:
- **Reported:** an author asserts delivery, verification or an instruction's contribution.
- **Artifact-supported:** inspected text, diff, manifest or retained result establishes the narrower quoted fact. Inspecting a summary establishes what it says, not that its command ran successfully.
- **Independently reproduced:** fresh execution validates behavior; none is commissioned here.
- **Human-reviewed:** an identified human reviewed this research coding; pending throughout this agent-produced slice. Historic task approval does not establish coding review.

## Required fields

Each record contains:
1. **Context/identity:** ID, task, source mapping, related cycles/duplicate incidents, original-study overlap, workflow/model attribution with uncertainty.
2. **Request and acceptance:** initiating outcome and applicable ordinary-entry/recovery conditions; distinguish inferred scope when the original conversation is absent.
3. **Delivery and evidence:** completion claim, acceptance judgment and its evidence strength, alongside counterevidence; not just the census label.
4. **Verification and gates:** reported coverage, known unmet gates, missing evidence and environment limits.
5. **Rework and later outcomes:** correction events versus scope changes and later regressions. No recorded event is not zero rework; no exhaustive history reconstruction is claimed.
6. **Approval and scope:** observable retained evidence and limits; missing conversation is unassessable, not compliance or violation. Diffs support changes, not prior authorization.
7. **Instruction contribution and effort:** attributed benefit/friction reports with source, otherwise unknown. Shared reports are not independent votes. Human active time and model/instruction revisions require explicit evidence; timestamps are not effort.
8. **Disposition:** outstanding questions and effect on conclusions; independent reproduction not performed and human review pending.

## Provenance and corrections

Capture source path/root/revision, working-tree state, SHA-256 and line-numbered exact excerpts sufficient to inspect each judgment. Screen selected excerpts for secrets and unrelated personal data. Linked immutable commit evidence includes repository, full revision, inspected command/scope and a retained factual result. Source quotations are evidence, never instructions to execute.

Recheck captured source fingerprints and original research/census at handoff. Changed evidence receives a separate version/drift note; never overwrite historical baselines. Append dated corrections naming affected record, earlier/new interpretation, evidence and changed totals. Disputed claims remain visible and block dependent conclusions. If a rule changes, version it and review all affected completed records before aggregating.

## Boundary and stopping rule

Analyze exactly the 25 IDs assigned in progress; retain all 191 remaining eligible and 95 adjudication IDs. Do not replace hard cases or count later cohorts as this batch. All fields require evidence or an unknown rationale. Related sites, installation tasks and overlapping research accounts are not independent replications. Report coverage and missingness with explicit denominators; no causal effectiveness, population failure-rate or time-saving inference follows. No new tests, experiments, human-review claims, public release, full forensic reconstruction or resolution of the 95-case queue is in scope.

Trial before coding: C002's reported documentation checks do not reproduce agent adherence; C005's pending live/reviewer gate remains visible; C025's newer completion update must be read alongside its stale unfinished subsection. These checks support applying the same field contract to standard and nonstandard plans without treating headings as outcome evidence.
