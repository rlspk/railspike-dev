## Identity and batches

A candidate is a retained document family; a canonical cycle is one attributable workflow. Assign each candidate exactly one disposition: canonical cycle; duplicate/variant linked to a canonical cycle; contextual planning excluded as an independent cycle; or unresolved identity. Record the reason. Use source hashes and request/provenance comparisons, not similar names alone. Copies contribute neither extra cycle counts nor independent evidence. Divergent copies retain variant identity and uncertainty unless common lineage is supported. Several slices in one roadmap do not establish several executed cycles.

## Status and evidence contract

- **Documented completion:** retained summary and supporting plan/record describe delivered scope and verification without a known outstanding mandatory completion condition in the screened evidence. This is documentary classification, not rerun verification or certification of every approval gate.
- **Completion with unmet gates:** completion is claimed but evidence explicitly leaves a required condition unmet. Optional follow-ups, out-of-scope features and later regressions are recorded separately; they do not automatically invalidate historical completion.
- **In-progress:** an affirmative record of work begun or work still under way. A plan's future-tense instructions alone do not prove implementation began.
- **Explicitly abandoned/superseded:** a source explicitly establishes disposition, rather than the collector inferring it from age, archive location, or absent steps.
- **Indeterminate:** evidence does not establish one of the above, or a conflict cannot be resolved. Missing summaries, missing approval conversations, and optional Step 1 are not automatic failures.

Each canonical record gives source mapping, task type, supported workflow era or unknown (exact instruction revision is unknown unless evidenced), status/rationale, evidence availability, original-study overlap, and any later-regression note. Preserve the source's claims as claims. File/section presence is corroborated by inspection; historical command results are reported unless actual retained results are inspected. No application tests, human effort measurements, or causal-effect estimates are performed.

Tool-assisted screening extracts goals, stage coverage, completion/verification statements, uncertainty and correction passages. Review these together; ambiguous or contradictory cases require targeted source reading or explicit indeterminate status. Do not describe excerpt screening as a full forensic review. Canonical records retain short exact excerpts with source IDs and original line numbers; full local texts are not copied into this publication. Fingerprints identify bytes but cannot reconstruct missing uncommitted source files.

## Eligibility, preservation and corrections

Retain research cycles in the inventory but exclude them from their own outcome analysis. Queue both documented-completion categories for later outcome analysis; incomplete/indeterminate records and unclear identity/eligibility go into a separate adjudication queue. All eligible cycles must be queued, not merely convenient examples. Cross-reference original study IDs without treating the old study as independent evidence.

Keep original sources and the original study unchanged. Record capture revisions, hashes and relevant excerpts. Recheck fingerprints at handoff; concurrent source changes are drift relative to this cutoff, not permission to alter the baseline. This census's own planning/summary changes are authorized self-record updates, excluded from outcome analysis. Future corrections append a dated record identifying old/new interpretation, evidence and affected totals; do not silently replace the frozen records. Missing or unavailable supporting evidence remains a reported limitation.
