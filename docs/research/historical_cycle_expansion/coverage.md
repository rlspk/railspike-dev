## Gaps and limits

- This is a local retained-file census: deleted/unretained cycles, private approval conversations, inaccessible original runtime transcripts, and non-Markdown/non-text workflow records may be missing. No global Git-history or remote-account completeness claim is made.
- Tool-assisted excerpt screening and targeted rereads establish document status, not a full forensic audit of every source. Historical tests/commands were not rerun. A passing focused test, shell command listed under Verification, or summary heading does not certify all gates.
- Many older plans have implementation notes but no explicit final verification outcome. They remain in adjudication; zero explicitly abandoned cycles does not mean all were continued.
- Exact agent/model versions, instruction revisions, elapsed active human effort and independent human coding review are generally unavailable. Era labels cite only retained text/path evidence.
- Source fingerprints and short exact excerpts preserve attribution, not full recovery of mutable uncommitted documents. Full original-study snapshots stay in the unchanged earlier evidence register. Apply the [correction procedure](./protocol.md#eligibility-preservation-and-corrections) if identities or outcomes are disputed.
- Research self-exclusion, installed-copy deduplication and separate context/identity counts prevent simple document totals from being presented as independent trials. No causal effectiveness or comparative performance conclusion follows from this census.
