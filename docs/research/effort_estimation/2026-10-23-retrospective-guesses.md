## How to use these estimates

All numbers use R1 and estimate total scoped work from the recorded cycle starting point, including inspection, planning, implementation, verification, documentation and ordinary correction. They are retrospective guesses, not measured labor, remaining effort or independent ratings. The ranges express subjective plausibility, not calibrated confidence intervals.

A row is one judgment about one scope. Its related checkpoint labels locate gaps; they do not turn that one judgment into several observations. Do not use these values in prospective forecast-accuracy calculations, add them across parent/child scopes, or count them as completed or accepted outcomes. Missing dates, hours, original approvals, actual outcomes and maintainer fit/acceptance remain unknown. Future evidence or revised judgments should be appended as new records referring to these IDs.

## Coverage and uncertainty

G01–G19 supply numerical judgments for nine cycles whose numeric scope estimates were unavailable in the captured records. Superseded retraction, pagination, visibility and checkout scopes remain separate. For a completed slice, the value describes its known scoped work; it does not pretend to be the earlier missing projection or manufacture a second independent closeout rating.

G20–G23 are more speculative scenarios for missing early checkpoints: composer focus/layout, dependency removal, list structure and documentation publication policy. Their known later scopes or scores inform the guesses. Earlier scope was not independently recovered; these values cannot establish what an intake estimate would have been. Other existing scores are retained rather than guessed again. Future, unstarted checkpoints are not missing historical observations.

The original clone review counted 18 P2 cycle headings. It also retained inline records for documentation publication policy, independent publication and feature-flag cleanup. This supplement includes the missing pre-inspection intake scenario for publication policy; the other two have recorded numeric intake/handoff values. The ledger heading count is not a complete population count.
