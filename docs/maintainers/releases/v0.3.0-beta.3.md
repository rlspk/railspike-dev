# Railspike v0.3.0 beta 3

Estimates are easier to scan, and agents attribute their plan contributions with verified model/settings when available.

## Changes

- Each estimated option/stage has a standalone bold estimate line directly beneath its heading. The cycle estimate appears near the top of the artifact. Use plain numbers without approximation signs; keep drivers, uncertainty and metadata separate. Comparison/history tables use a dedicated estimate column.
- Original, revised and retrospective values stay separately labeled with retained history. Missing and unavailable values remain explicit. Estimate meanings, R1 and mode defaults are unchanged.
- Agents sign beneath their authored/revised contributions with their name and UTC date. Include model/version and relevant settings such as reasoning effort or temperature when verified for that contribution. Omit unknown fields rather than guessing or delaying work.
- Preserve earlier signatures and append dated “Revised by” attribution on revisions, including changed model/settings. Attribution applies across Steps 1–4 independently of estimate/debug mode. It is textual authorship, not cryptographic signing, approval or an independent rating.

## Update and recovery
