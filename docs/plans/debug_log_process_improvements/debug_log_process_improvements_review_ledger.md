### How to avoid repeat review

Before opening content, rescan both roots and compare path plus SHA-256 with this ledger. Skip unchanged reviewed content; recognize moved/copied files by hash and preserve their new path mapping. For an appended log, compare the previously reviewed byte prefix with its recorded hash, then inspect the addition and enough context to interpret it. If the prefix differs, review the changed content against the frozen source. Append a dated review record with new hash, byte/line extent, findings and disposition; never replace this baseline. Missing files are missing, not newly reviewed. Scan for new filenames and rotations on every pass. A changed instruction contract can require reassessing conclusions even when source bytes are unchanged.
