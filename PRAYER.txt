Gracious God, our Creator and faithful guide, we thank You for this beginning
and dedicate this project to the service of our neighbors.

You promise wisdom to those who ask (James 1:5); grant all who build, use, or
encounter this project discernment to choose what is good, humility to
acknowledge mistakes, and courage to make them right.

Help us work with honesty and care, welcome people of every faith and
background, and place the well-being of others at the heart of what we create.

Establish the work of our hands (Psalm 90:17), let it bear fruit in
understanding and peace, and guide our plans according to Your will.

Amen.
